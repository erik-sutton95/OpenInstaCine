Since open beta build 26

New and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls are closer to the other OpenCine apps, with the same look and behavior.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting clips and Select all stay responsive, even while clips cache.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting, because the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tapping the storage readout steps through free space, free percentage and the camera's remaining record time, then back to free space.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. In portrait, readouts use a three-column grid and the format readout lines up with the clock.
- Offline, Media says the camera is disconnected and shows the clips cached on this phone. An empty filter says No matching clips.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- The Local Network prompt appears during pairing, before the camera Wi-Fi join, and Allow carries on pairing without relaunching the app.
- After a failed saved connection, returning to the app allows another attempt.
- Reconnecting waits while the app is in the background, so the live picture can return without Retry. The card names its 3-minute reconnect window.
- A stalled picture gets a bounded recovery instead of staying on Reconnecting. When the link drops, the signal shows no bars.
- If the live picture's decoder fails, the picture recovers at the next keyframe instead of staying frozen.
- Returning from Media refreshes the live picture instead of leaving an old frame on screen.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual changes, AE lock and frame-rate changes again. Auto ISO with a fixed shutter reads M.
- Playback stays paused after Share, buffering, or leaving and returning to the app.
- Sharing several cached clips works together. A clip that fails to cache says so, and Cache or Share carries on with the others.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip finishes downloading after you close it and reads CACHED.
- Monitoring colour follows the selected camera profile, and I-Log highlight warnings account for ISO.
- Using a volume button as the remote shutter is protected against a rare unexpected quit.
- VoiceOver no longer reaches live controls under a covering screen or the hidden buttons of a collapsed landscape palette.
- The open landscape View Assist palette hides the camera values it covers. Portrait card-space labels keep their full value near the cutout.

What to test

- Open Media on a full card. Scroll, select clips, use Select all, then Cache and Share two clips. Check the screen stays responsive and both clips finish or report a failure.
- Delete and reinstall, then pair: the Local Network prompt comes before the Wi-Fi Join alert, and Allow continues to live view.
- During Live View, go Home for over 3 minutes and return: the picture comes back without Retry. Then open Media, play a clip and return to Live View.
- At 180 degrees and 25p, change Auto to Manual, then 25p to 50p. Check the shutter follows from 1/50 to 1/100.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip a few seconds, go back and wait about 30 s: it reads CACHED. In video mode, tap the storage readout through all three steps.
