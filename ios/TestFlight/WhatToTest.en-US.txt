New and changed

- In landscape, the colour profile now appears beside the codec in the top bar.

Fixes

- Returning from Media refreshes the live picture instead of leaving an old frame on screen.
- After a failed connection, returning to the app allows another connection attempt.
- Portrait card-space labels keep their full rounded value near the screen cutout.

What to test

- Open Media, play a clip and return to Live View. Check that the live picture resumes.
- Rotate between portrait and landscape. Check the codec, colour profile and free card space stay readable.
- After a failed connection, leave and return to the app, then retry connecting.
