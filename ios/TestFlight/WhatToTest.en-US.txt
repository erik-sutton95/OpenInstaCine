Since open beta build 26

New and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls look and behave like the other OpenCine apps.
- Cameras > Add setup can connect a Luna over your Wi-Fi network or this phone's hotspot. Camera Wi-Fi stays as the other setup.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting clips and Select all stay responsive, even while clips cache.
- Media grid is staggered: portrait photos stand upright and every item fills the space above it.
- Caching and downloads show a live speed beside the percentage, for example 18% and 42 MB/s.
- Selecting clips shows an Exit selection close beside the count, then All, Share and Delete.
- Pinch-zoomed playback now fills the screen past the clip's letterbox, up to the screen edge.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting: the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tap the storage readout to step through free space, free percentage and the camera's remaining record time.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec, and Photo mode setup values move to the top corner so captions fit.
- In portrait, readouts use a three-column grid and the format lines up with the clock.
- Offline, Media shows the clips cached on this phone; an empty filter says No matching clips.
- Diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate picture interruptions.

Fixes

- Connecting no longer freezes the app on Starting live view or Opening the video link.
- The Local Network prompt comes during pairing, before the camera Wi-Fi join, and Allow carries on without relaunching the app.
- After a failed saved connection, returning to the app allows another attempt.
- Reconnecting waits while the app is in the background, so the picture returns without Retry. The card names its 3-minute window.
- A stalled picture gets a bounded recovery, a failed decoder recovers at the next keyframe, and a dropped link shows no bars.
- Returning from Media refreshes the live picture instead of leaving an old frame on screen.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual changes, AE lock and frame-rate changes again. Auto ISO with a fixed shutter reads M.
- Playback stays paused after Share, buffering, or leaving and returning to the app.
- Playback stays smoother on large cards, scopes update more evenly, and wide clips keep correct brightness through LUTs and scopes.
- Sharing several cached clips works together. A clip that fails to cache says so, and Cache or Share carries on with the others.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip keeps downloading after you close it and reads CACHED.
- Sharing a clip you left mid-download in the player, or whose download stopped, finishes its copy instead of failing.
- Monitoring colour follows the selected camera profile, and I-Log highlight warnings account for ISO.
- The volume-button remote shutter ignores late volume changes and no longer causes a rare unexpected quit.
- VoiceOver no longer reaches live controls under a covering screen or the hidden buttons of a collapsed landscape palette.
- Portrait card-space labels keep their full value near the cutout.

What to test

- On a full card, select clips, Share two, then Exit selection. Play a clip, pinch to zoom and pan to the screen edges.
- Add a Wi-Fi or Hotspot setup for the Luna under Cameras > Add setup and connect with it, then switch back to Camera Wi-Fi.
- Reinstall and pair: Local Network comes before the Wi-Fi Join alert. Disconnect and connect again: live view starts without a freeze.
- During Live View, go Home for over 3 minutes and return: the picture comes back without Retry. Then play a clip and return.
- At 180 degrees/25p, change Auto to Manual, then 50p: the shutter follows from 1/50 to 1/100. With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip briefly, go back and Share it.
