New and changed

- Rebuilt live view, camera browsing and settings on OpenCine, the shared foundation of the OpenCapture apps.
- Luna camera and gimbal tools retain their supported options. ISO, shutter, white balance and Record still wait for the camera to confirm.
- For Luna Ultra on firmware 1.1.15. Live audio controls and meters remain unavailable.

Fixes

- Camera browsing now includes internal storage and a present SD card, instead of omitting SD clips.
- The camera gallery returns after a connection recovery, so uncached clips remain available when live view has returned.
- Playback stays paused when a late loop, seek or scrub finishes. Press Play to resume.
- Fixed missing privacy information that blocked TestFlight uploads.

What to test

- Force quit the Insta360 app, pair and check live view. Change ISO, shutter and WB, then record from the app, camera and Apple Watch. Check the camera confirms each change.
- Open clips from internal storage and an SD card. Record a short take, reopen the gallery, pause during looping or scrubbing, then check cached playback offline.
- Try scopes, false color, peaking and an I-Log LUT in both orientations. Open and close assist options, then try zoom, the gimbal and AirPods head tracking.
