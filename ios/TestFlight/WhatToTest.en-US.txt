New and changed

- Go live streams the monitor picture to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or a Custom server over a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup. Set it up in Settings > Sharing > Live stream.
- Hold the zoom chip for one zoom panel: a ruler for smooth zoom, three presets and a preset ramp. Go live now sits under the timecode.
- Multiview shows up to four Lunas on one shared network. Camera presets save exposure and white balance, and the lock button also holds rotation.

Fixes

- On a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup, a clip still on the camera says it plays over Camera Wi-Fi instead of Could not open that clip, and Share and Save skip it.
- On Luna firmware 1.2.7, a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup keeps the camera on the network: no picture drop every couple of minutes, and Go live keeps its bitrate.
- Zoom preset ramps move smoothly instead of in visible steps.
- A zoom step the camera misses no longer stops the ramp or says Zoom was not applied.

What to test

- On a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup, open a clip that is not on the phone: the player explains, and thumbnails still load. On Camera Wi-Fi the clip plays.
- On a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup, save a stream key in Settings > Sharing > Live stream, Go live for 10 minutes, then Stop. The picture and stream stay up.
- Hold the zoom chip: drag the ruler, save three presets, set a ramp and tap between presets. The zoom moves smoothly.
