New and changed

- Camera controls and Media now use the shared OpenCine screens, so they look and behave as in the other OpenCine camera apps.
- Exposure, shutter angle, frame-rate and storage readouts follow the camera's reports; missing measurements are marked unavailable.
- Live View and Media use the connected camera's available controls; playback controls show whether a clip is playing or waiting for data.

Fixes

- A format change rematches shutter angle only when manual exposure and video timing are known.
- Unknown exposure state no longer triggers a hidden shutter change.
- In portrait, the status readouts use a three-column grid that grows with the number of rows.

What to test

- Connect your camera, switch manual and automatic exposure, then change resolution or frame rate and compare the shutter with the camera screen.
- Check storage with and without a memory card, then reconnect and open Media before returning to Live View.
- Rotate the phone and check the controls and how missing readings are shown.
- Rotate to portrait and check that every status readout is visible in the grid.
