New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual changes, AE lock and frame-rate changes again.
- Returning from Media refreshes the live picture. Playback stays paused after buffering or leaving and returning to the app.
- Sharing several cached clips works together. Monitoring colour follows the selected camera profile. I-Log warnings account for ISO; their ceilings still need physical calibration.
- Returning after a failed saved connection allows another attempt. A stalled picture has bounded recovery instead of remaining on Reconnecting indefinitely.

What to test

- At 180 degrees and 25p, change Auto to Manual, then 25p to 50p. Check the shutter follows from 1/50 to 1/100.
- In Media, pause and scrub a clip, switch clips, cancel a deletion and share cached clips. Return to Live View and check that the live picture resumes.
- If the live picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time, what you were doing and whether camera controls still responded.
