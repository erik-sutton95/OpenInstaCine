New and changed

- ISO, shutter and white balance show as set only once the camera reports them. A change the camera drops says it was not applied instead of quietly reverting.
- Record sends start or stop for what the camera reports, never a blind toggle, and waits for the camera's capture status right after connecting.
- First open beta: a free, open-source field monitor for the Insta360 Luna Ultra (firmware 1.1.15) with live view, scopes, false color, zebras, peaking and LUTs.

Fixes

- The zoom chip and dial now reach and show 15x. The readout used to stop at 12x.
- AirPods head tracking follows your head smoothly instead of jumping, and the gimbal stops as soon as you stop moving.
- The EV assist reads the live picture, so auto exposure shows about 0.0 instead of -1.3.

- Playback stays paused when a late loop, seek or scrub finishes. Press Play to resume.

What to test

- Set ISO Auto, 400 and 800, then change shutter and ISO quickly: each settles on the camera's value within a few seconds and never bounces back. Try WB Kelvin and Auto.
- Start and stop recording from the app, the camera button and Apple Watch: the lamp follows the camera. Drop Wi-Fi during a change: it says the link dropped.
- Force quit the Insta360 app, pair from the start screen, zoom the chip and dial to 15x. Try waveform, false color and an I-Log LUT. Pause a clip during looping or scrubbing: it should stay paused.
