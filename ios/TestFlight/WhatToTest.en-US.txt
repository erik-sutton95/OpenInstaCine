New and changed

- In portrait, the large format readout now lines up exactly with the clock at the top of the screen.

Fixes

- With Shutter Angle on, picking an angle, switching Auto to Manual and AE lock set the shutter again, and Auto ISO with a fixed shutter reads M.
- With Shutter Angle on, changing the frame rate keeps the chosen angle.
- If the live picture's decoder fails, the picture recovers at the next keyframe instead of staying frozen.

What to test

- Turn on Shutter Angle, pick 180 degrees at 25p, then switch EXPOSURE Auto and back to Manual; check the camera follows with no "not applied" note.
- With Angle at 180 degrees, change 4K25 to 4K50 and check the shutter becomes 1/100.
- Rotate to portrait and check that the format readout and the clock share the same top edge.
