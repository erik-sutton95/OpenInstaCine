New and changed

- First open beta: a free, open-source field monitor for the Insta360 Luna Ultra (firmware 1.1.15) with live view, scopes, false color, zebras, peaking and LUTs.
- Full camera control: exposure, white balance, METER (Matrix or Face), focus, recording and timecode, plus gimbal joystick, Recenter and AirPods head tracking.
- Double-tap the picture to start Deep Track. Long-press the focus box to lock it so a stray tap cannot move it.

Fixes

- AirPods head tracking now follows your head smoothly instead of jumping, and the gimbal stops as soon as you stop moving.
- The EV assist now reads the live picture, so auto exposure shows about 0.0 instead of -1.3.

What to test

- Force quit the Insta360 app, then pair from the start screen and allow Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Local Network. Pairing should reach live view.
- Start and stop a recording, check the timecode, and try waveform, false color and a LUT on I-Log.
- Move the gimbal with the joystick and AirPods, double-tap a subject to track it, then report anything off with a TestFlight screenshot.
