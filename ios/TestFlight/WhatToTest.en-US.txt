New features
- OpenInstaCine is a free, open-source field monitor for the Insta360 Luna Ultra: live view, scopes, exposure assists, recording and gimbal control from your iPhone.
- It needs a Luna Ultra on camera firmware 1.1.15. Force quit the Insta360 app first, then pair from the start screen and allow Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Local Network access.
- Try pairing, live view with waveform and false colour, starting and stopping a recording, zoom, and moving the gimbal. Double-tap the picture to start Deep Track.
- Something wrong? Take a screenshot in TestFlight and paste the copied report into your feedback, or use Report a problem in Operator Setup. Email support@opencapture.org.
