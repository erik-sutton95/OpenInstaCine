New and changed

- The live top bar readouts (storage, format, mode, REC and timecode) get their dark glow back, so they stay readable over bright pictures.

Fixes

- With two or more frame guides and Mask outside frame on, the space between the crossing frames stays clear again; only the area outside their combined bounding rectangle is darkened.

What to test

- Turn on Guides, select 2.39:1 and 9:16, switch on Mask outside frame and check the corners between the two frames stay bright.
- Point the camera at a bright scene and check every top bar readout stays readable in portrait and landscape.
- Record a short clip, open Media and return to Live View; report crashes or controls that stop responding.
