New and changed

- Rebuilt live view, camera browsing and settings on OpenCine, the shared foundation of the OpenCapture apps.
- Luna camera and gimbal tools retain their supported options. ISO, shutter, white balance and Record still wait for the camera to confirm.
- For Luna Ultra on firmware 1.1.15; Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone. Live audio controls and meters remain unavailable.

Fixes

- Camera browsing now includes internal storage and a present SD card, instead of omitting SD clips.
- Playback stays paused when a late loop, seek or scrub finishes. Press Play to resume.
- Live View shows a vertical camera picture whole instead of a heavily zoomed crop.
- Updated full-screen display setup for Android 15 and later.

What to test

- Force quit the Insta360 app, pair and check live view. Change ISO, shutter and WB, then record from the app and camera. Check the camera confirms each change.
- Check portrait and landscape with scopes, false color and peaking. Open and close assist options, try zoom and the gimbal, and compare clip assists with live view.
- Open clips from internal storage and an SD card. Record a short take, reopen the gallery and pause during loops or scrubs. Report problems from Operator Setup or support@opencapture.org.
