Since open beta build 26

New and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls look and behave like the other OpenCine apps.
- Cameras > Add setup can connect a Luna over your Wi-Fi network or this phone's hotspot. Camera Wi-Fi stays as the other setup.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting clips and Select all stay responsive, even while clips cache.
- Media grid is staggered: portrait photos stand upright and every item fills the space above it.
- Caching and downloads show a live speed beside the percentage, for example 18% and 42 MB/s.
- Selecting clips shows an Exit selection close beside the count, then All, Share and Delete. Media's top corner is now a Back arrow.
- Pinch-zoomed playback now fills the screen past the clip's letterbox, up to the screen edge.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting: the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tap the storage readout to step through free space, free percentage and the camera's remaining record time.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec, and Photo mode setup values move to the top corner so captions fit.
- Offline, Media says the camera is disconnected and shows the clips cached on this phone. An empty filter says No matching clips.
- Diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate picture interruptions.

Fixes

- The app no longer freezes or shows App not responding while the live picture rebuilds, for example after rotating or returning.
- Reconnecting waits in the background and resumes on return without Retry. The card names its 3-minute window; a dropped link shows no bars.
- A failed saved connection can retry when you return. Scanning messages follow Bluetooth availability, and Wi-Fi failures offer the right settings.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual changes, AE lock and frame-rate changes again. Auto ISO with a fixed shutter reads M.
- The EV meter reads the live picture again instead of always showing a dash.
- Playback keeps Pause through buffering, scrubbing and Share.
- Playback stays smoother on large cards; scopes and audio meters update without redrawing the whole player.
- Sharing two or more cached clips no longer fails with Couldn't share these clips; they reach the share sheet together.
- A clip that fails to cache shows Could not cache that clip, and Cache or Share carries on with the others. The selection bar shows the whole count.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip keeps downloading after you close it and reads CACHED. Shared clips keep camera file names.
- Sharing a clip you left mid-download in the player, or whose download stopped, finishes its copy instead of failing.
- With two or more frame guides and Mask outside frame on, the space between the crossing frames stays clear again.
- TalkBack reads each settings switch as one switch with its title.

What to test

- On a full card, select clips, Share two, then Exit selection. Play a clip, pinch to zoom and pan to the screen edges.
- Add a Wi-Fi or Hotspot setup for the Luna under Cameras > Add setup and connect with it, then switch back to Camera Wi-Fi.
- Rotate the phone repeatedly and leave and return to the app during Live View: the app stays responsive and the picture returns.
- At 180 degrees and 25p, change Auto to Manual, then 25p to 50p. Check 1/50 becomes 1/100 and the EV needle follows the scene.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip briefly, go back and Share it. Turn on Guides 2.39:1 and 9:16 with Mask outside frame.
