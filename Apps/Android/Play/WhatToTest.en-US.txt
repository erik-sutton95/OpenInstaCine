New and changed

- Playback controls follow the player's real playing and buffering state.
- For Luna Ultra on firmware 1.1.15; Android 10+ on a 64-bit phone. Live audio controls and meters remain unavailable.

Fixes

- Pause remains available while playback waits for data; pressing it prevents an unexpected restart.
- Playback stays paused when a late loop, seek or scrub finishes. Press Play to resume.
- Camera browsing includes internal storage and a present SD card.

What to test

- Reconnect your camera, open Media and try short and long clips from internal storage and an SD card. Check cached playback offline.
- Pause while a clip is buffering, then scrub or switch clips. Playback should restart only when you press Play.
- Check Media and playback in portrait and landscape. If a clip will not open, report its duration, storage and whether it was cached.
