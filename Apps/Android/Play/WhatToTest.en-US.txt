New and changed

- Exposure, shutter angle and frame-rate readouts follow values reported by the camera.
- Storage readouts preserve reported precision and mark missing measurements as unavailable.
- Live View and Media use the connected camera's available controls.

Fixes

- Fixed ML Kit initialization in minified Android releases to prevent a crash when live video starts.
- Pause remains available while playback waits for data; using it prevents unexpected restarts. Late loops, seeks and scrubs stay paused until you press Play.
- Camera browsing includes internal storage and a present SD card.
- A format change rematches shutter angle only when manual exposure and video timing are known; unknown exposure state never triggers a hidden shutter change.

What to test

- Connect your camera, keep live view running and try face tracking. Switch manual and automatic exposure, change resolution or frame rate and compare the shutter with the camera screen.
- Check storage with and without a memory card, then reconnect. In Media, try short and long clips from internal storage and an SD card, including cached playback offline; pause while buffering, scrub and switch clips. Playback should restart only when you press Play.
- Rotate the phone; check controls and missing readings in Live View, Media and playback, then return from Media to Live View. Report any crash after the first picture. If a clip will not open, report its duration, storage and whether it was cached.
