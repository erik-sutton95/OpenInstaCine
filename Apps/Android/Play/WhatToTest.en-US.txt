New and changed

- Exposure, shutter angle and frame-rate readouts follow values reported by the camera.
- Storage readouts preserve reported precision and mark missing measurements as unavailable.
- Live View and Media use the connected camera's available controls.

Fixes

- Fixed ML Kit initialization in minified Android releases to prevent a crash when live video starts.
- Pause remains available while playback waits for data; using it prevents unexpected restarts. Late loops, seeks and scrubs stay paused until you press Play.
- Camera browsing includes internal storage and a present SD card.
- A format change rematches shutter angle only when manual exposure and video timing are known; unknown exposure state never triggers a hidden shutter change.

What to test

- Connect your camera; try live view and face tracking. Change manual/auto exposure, resolution or frame rate; compare shutter with its screen. Report crashes after the first picture.
- Check storage with/without an SD card; try short/long clips on both stores and cached playback offline. Pause while buffering, scrub and switch clips; restart only with Play.
- Reconnect, rotate and open Media/playback; check controls and missing readings, then return to Live View. If a clip will not open, report duration, storage and whether it was cached.
