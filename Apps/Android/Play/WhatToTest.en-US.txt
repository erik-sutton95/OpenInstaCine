New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- The app no longer freezes or shows App not responding while the live picture rebuilds, for example after rotating or returning to the app.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual changes, AE lock and frame-rate changes again. The EV meter reads the live picture.
- Playback keeps Pause through buffering, scrubbing and Share. Sharing several cached clips reaches the share sheet together.
- A failed saved connection can retry when you return. Top-bar readouts stay readable, and crossing frame guides stay clear with Mask outside frame.

What to test

- Rotate the phone repeatedly and leave and return to the app during Live View. Check the app stays responsive and the live picture returns.
- At 180 degrees and 25p, change Auto to Manual, then 25p to 50p. Check 1/50 becomes 1/100 and the EV needle responds to bright and dark scenes.
- Share several cached clips and pause while buffering. If the live picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time.
