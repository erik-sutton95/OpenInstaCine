New and changed

- ISO, shutter and white balance show as set only once the camera reports them. A change the camera drops says it was not applied instead of quietly reverting.
- Record sends start or stop for what the camera reports, never a blind toggle, and waits for the camera's capture status right after connecting.
- OpenInstaCine is a free, open-source field monitor for the Insta360 Luna Ultra on firmware 1.1.15. It needs a 64-bit phone on Android 10 or newer.

Fixes

- Live View shows a vertical camera picture whole instead of a heavily zoomed crop.
- Playback scopes, zebra and false color keep their own clip level instead of moving the live picture's, and the reverse.

What to test

- Force quit the Insta360 app, then pair from the start screen. Set ISO Auto, 400 and 800 and WB Kelvin: each settles on the camera's value without bouncing back.
- Start and stop recording from the app and the camera button: the lamp follows the camera. Drop Wi-Fi during a change: it says the link dropped, then controls work again.
- Try waveform, false color, zoom and the gimbal. Something wrong? Use Report a problem in Operator Setup, or email support@opencapture.org.
