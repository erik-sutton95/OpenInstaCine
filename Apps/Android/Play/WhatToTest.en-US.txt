New and changed

- Share several cached clips at once from Media; they reach the share sheet together.

Fixes

- The EV meter reads the live picture again instead of always showing a dash.
- With Shutter Angle on, picking an angle, switching Auto to Manual and AE lock set the shutter again, and Auto ISO with a fixed shutter reads M.
- With Shutter Angle on, changing the frame rate keeps the chosen angle.
- Sharing two or more cached clips no longer fails with "Couldn't share these clips."
- The live top bar readouts (storage, format, mode, REC and timecode) keep their dark glow, so they stay readable over bright pictures.
- With two or more frame guides and Mask outside frame on, the space between the crossing frames stays clear again; only the area outside their combined bounding rectangle is darkened.

What to test

- Turn on the EV tool and point the camera at bright and dark scenes; the meter needle should move.
- Turn on Shutter Angle, pick 180 degrees at 25p, switch EXPOSURE Auto and back to Manual, then change 4K25 to 4K50; the shutter should follow (1/50, then 1/100).
- Cache two or more clips, select them in Media and share. Check that all of them reach the app you pick.
- Turn on Guides, select 2.39:1 and 9:16, switch on Mask outside frame and check the corners between the two frames stay bright.
- Point the camera at a bright or white scene and check every top bar readout stays readable in portrait and landscape.
