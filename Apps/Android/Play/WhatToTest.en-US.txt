Since open beta build 26

New and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls are closer to the other OpenCine apps, with the same look and behavior.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting clips and Select all stay responsive, even while clips cache.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting, because the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tapping the storage readout steps through free space, free percentage and the camera's remaining record time, then back to free space.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Offline, Media says the camera is disconnected and shows the clips cached on this phone. An empty filter says No matching clips.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- The app no longer freezes or shows App not responding while the live picture rebuilds, for example after rotating or returning to the app.
- Reconnecting waits while the app is in the background and resumes when you return, so the live picture comes back without Retry.
- The reconnect card names its 3-minute window. When the link drops, the signal shows no bars and TalkBack says the live link is down.
- A failed saved connection can retry when you return. Scanning messages follow Bluetooth availability, and Wi-Fi failures offer the right settings.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual changes, AE lock and frame-rate changes again. Auto ISO with a fixed shutter reads M.
- The EV meter reads the live picture again instead of always showing a dash.
- Playback keeps Pause through buffering, scrubbing and Share.
- Sharing two or more cached clips no longer fails with Couldn't share these clips; they reach the share sheet together.
- A clip that fails to cache now shows Could not cache that clip, and Cache or Share carries on with the others. The selection bar shows the whole count.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip finishes downloading after you close it and reads CACHED. Shared clips keep their camera file names.
- With two or more frame guides and Mask outside frame on, the space between the crossing frames stays clear again.
- The open landscape View Assist palette hides the camera values it covers. TalkBack reads each settings switch as one switch with its title.

What to test

- Open Media on a full card. Scroll, select clips, use Select all, then Cache and Share two clips. Check the screen stays responsive and both clips finish or report a failure.
- Rotate the phone repeatedly and leave and return to the app during Live View. Check the app stays responsive and the live picture returns.
- In Live View, go Home, record 60 to 90 s with the system camera app, then return: the picture comes back by itself with no Retry.
- At 180 degrees and 25p, change Auto to Manual, then 25p to 50p. Check 1/50 becomes 1/100 and the EV needle responds to bright and dark scenes.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip a few seconds, go back and wait about 30 s: it reads CACHED. Turn on Guides 2.39:1 and 9:16 with Mask outside frame.
