New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual changes, AE lock and frame-rate changes again. The EV meter reads the live picture.
- Playback keeps Pause through buffering and scrubbing. Sharing several cached clips reaches the share sheet together.
- Top-bar readouts stay readable over bright pictures, and the space between crossing frame guides stays clear with Mask outside frame.
- A failed connection can retry when you return. Scanning messages follow Bluetooth availability, and Wi-Fi failures offer the right settings.

What to test

- At 180 degrees and 25p, change Auto to Manual, then 25p to 50p. Check 1/50 becomes 1/100 and the EV needle responds to bright and dark scenes.
- Share several cached clips, pause while buffering, then check crossing guides, Mask outside frame and top-bar readability in both orientations.
- If the live picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time, what you were doing and whether camera controls still responded.
