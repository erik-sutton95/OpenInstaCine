This is the **first open beta** of OpenInstaCine, version **0.1.0** on both
platforms: a free, open-source field monitor for the **Insta360 Luna Ultra on
camera firmware 1.1.15**. Store build numbers differ between iOS and Android.
Availability is announced on the
[OpenInstaCine page](https://opencapture.org/openinstacine/).

iPhone and iPad are the daily driver. Android runs the same monitor against a
real Luna; a few iOS features are still missing there.

## iOS

### iOS: new and changed

- Live view with scopes, false color, zebras, peaking and LUTs, including the official Insta360 I-Log to Rec.709 look.
- Full camera control: exposure, white balance, METER (Matrix or Face), focus, recording and timecode, plus the gimbal joystick, Recenter and AirPods head tracking.
- Double-tap the picture to start Deep Track. Long-press the focus box to lock it so a stray tap cannot move it.

### iOS: fixes

- AirPods head tracking follows your head smoothly instead of jumping, and the gimbal stops as soon as you stop moving.
- The EV assist reads the live picture, so auto exposure shows about 0.0 instead of -1.3.

### iOS: what to test

- Force quit the Insta360 app, then pair from the start screen and allow Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and Local Network. Pairing should reach live view.
- Start and stop a recording, check the timecode, and try waveform, false color and a LUT on I-Log.
- Move the gimbal with the joystick and AirPods, double-tap a subject to track it, then report anything off with a TestFlight screenshot.

## Android

### Android: new and changed

- Live view, scopes, exposure assists, recording and gimbal control from your phone.
- Needs a 64-bit phone on Android 10 or newer.

### Android: what to test

- Force quit the Insta360 app, then pair from the start screen.
- Pairing, live view with waveform and false color, starting and stopping a recording, zoom, and moving the gimbal.

## Availability and remaining limits

- Not on the Luna: live audio control and meters (set audio on the camera),
  aperture, AE and AWB lock, Multiview, and Wi-Fi or Hotspot setups.
- Color Recovery is not in the app yet.
- Android does not have AirPods head tracking or the flat I-Log preview
  preference yet.

Something wrong? Use **Report a problem** in Operator Setup, or see
[opencapture.org/openinstacine/support](https://opencapture.org/openinstacine/support/).
