---
title: OpenInstaCine 0.2.0 release notes
description: iOS and Android changes for testers upgrading from 0.1.3, led by live streaming, the zoom panel and Luna firmware 1.2.7 fixes.
---

This beta covers the changes for testers moving from **0.1.3 to 0.2.0**. iOS and
Android cover the same release window; their store build numbers differ. It is a
new version, so the first external TestFlight build of 0.2.0 goes through
TestFlight Beta App Review before testers can install it.

Multiview and Android live streaming have not been checked with cameras yet. Read
them as what the app is built to do; reports are welcome.

## iOS

### iOS: new and changed

- Go live streams the monitor picture to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram or a Custom server. Set the service and stream key in Settings > Sharing > Live stream.
- Go live needs a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup; Camera Wi-Fi blocks it with the reason. A Custom server may use plain rtmp:// (for TikTok LIVE) with a warning.
- While the monitor is closed, a Live pill at the top shows the stream with Stop. Leaving the app ends the stream.
- When the camera picture is below 1080p, the Live stream card offers 1080p upscaled beside Camera feed.
- Hold the zoom chip for one zoom panel: the current zoom, a ruler for smooth zoom, three presets and a preset ramp from Off to 10 s.
- Go live sits under the timecode and steps aside while the zoom panel is open.
- Multiview shows up to four Lunas on one shared Wi-Fi network or this phone's hotspot. Open it from the grid icon on Your cameras ([Multiview](../../guides/multiview/)).
- Camera presets on Settings > Controls save the camera's exposure and white balance under a name, to recall later.
- GUIDES options add Action safe and Title safe lines with an adjustable size.
- The lock button also holds the screen rotation, upside-down landscape included.

### iOS: fixes

- On Luna firmware 1.2.7, a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup keeps the camera on the network: no picture drop every couple of minutes, and Go live keeps its bitrate.
- Zoom preset ramps move smoothly instead of in visible steps.
- A zoom step the camera misses no longer stops the ramp or says Zoom was not applied.
- Long programmed gimbal moves no longer stop partway with Programmed move was not applied.

### iOS: what to test

- On a Wi-Fi or hotspot setup, save a stream key in Settings > Sharing > Live stream, Go live for 10 minutes, then Stop. The picture and stream stay up.
- Hold the zoom chip: drag the ruler, save three presets, set a ramp and tap between presets. The zoom moves smoothly.
- Save a camera preset in Settings > Controls, change exposure and recall it. Lock the monitor upside down and turn the phone: it stays.
