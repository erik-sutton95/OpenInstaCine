OpenInstaCine is the open field monitor for the Insta360 Luna Ultra: scopes,
exposure assists, camera and gimbal control, and playback on your phone. These
pages are the **public handbook**: protocol, both apps, and how to build and
maintain the project. Markdown in `handbook/src/content/docs/` is the source.
Research logs and status ledgers stay in the git repository's
[`docs/`](https://github.com/erik-sutton95/OpenInstaCine/tree/main/docs) folder;
these pages summarize them.

Product page: [opencapture.org/openinstacine](https://opencapture.org/openinstacine/).
Privacy: [opencapture.org/openinstacine/privacy](https://opencapture.org/openinstacine/privacy/).
Help: [opencapture.org/openinstacine/support](https://opencapture.org/openinstacine/support/).

OpenInstaCine is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Insta360
(Arashi Vision Inc.). No Insta360 SDK or proprietary documentation is in this
repository. Protocol facts come from public community schemas, observed
behavior and the hardware we test.

:::caution[Do not publish captures]
Packet captures, camera Wi-Fi passwords and approval identities stay in the
gitignored `captures/` and `.local/` folders and are never committed. Do not
open issues with pcaps, passwords or identities.
:::

## Shared protocol

BLE is control and bootstrap only; control, live view and media go over the camera's own Wi-Fi.

```text
BLE scan → GATT connect → key exchange → owner approval → read Wi-Fi creds → join AP → TCP 6666 UCD2 → HTTP media
```
